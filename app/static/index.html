BE Final-Year Project Presentation Deck (v1 Prototype)

Key Presentation Talking Points for Evaluators:

  • Problem: Financial transaction fraud & money laundering is coordinated and dynamic; isolated tabular checks fail to catch network topology (smurfing & circular laundering).
  • Architecture: Accounts are nodes; transactions are timestamped directed edges. PyTorch Geometric TGN updates recurrent node state dynamically upon each event.
  • Integrity & Zero Leakage: Encoders, scalers and thresholds fit on train only. Test cutoff frozen on validation F1. No fake scores or inflated metrics reported.
  • Explainability: 200 cycles and 35 fan-out motifs automatically extracted strictly from historical preceding edges.
